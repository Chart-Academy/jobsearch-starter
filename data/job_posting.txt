Junior SOC Analyst (Tier 1)
Example Security Services · Washington, DC (on-site) · Full-time

About the role
Join our Security Operations Center to watch for and respond to security alerts
for our clients. You'll learn on the job from senior analysts.

What you'll do
- Monitor alerts in our SIEM and sort them by priority
- Investigate suspicious emails and report phishing
- Open, update, and close tickets with clear notes
- Escalate confirmed incidents to Tier 2 analysts
- Follow written procedures and help improve them

Requirements
- CompTIA Security+ (or able to earn it within 90 days of hire)
- Basic understanding of networking: TCP/IP, DNS, ports
- Familiarity with Windows and Active Directory account management
- Clear written communication
- Able to work rotating shifts

Nice to have
- Help desk or IT support experience
- Exposure to a SIEM such as Splunk or Microsoft Sentinel
